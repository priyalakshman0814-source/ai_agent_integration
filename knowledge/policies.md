# SOFT FLOWER POLICIES

## Returns
Customers may request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Products must be unopened and unused.

Products that have been opened or used may not qualify for return.

## Damaged Products
If a product arrives damaged, the customer should contact SOFT FLOWER customer support with their order details and photographs of the damaged product.

## Shipping
Orders are processed after successful payment.

Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days.

Delivery times may vary depending on location.

## Refunds
Approved refunds are processed after the returned product has been inspected.

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method.

## Cancellation
Orders may be cancelled before they are dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

## AI Policy Rule
The AI must never invent a policy.

If the knowledge base does not contain the answer, the AI should say that the information is not currently available and direct the customer to SOFT FLOWER support.
